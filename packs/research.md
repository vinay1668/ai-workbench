# Research pack

**Use when:** comparing tools, investigating a topic, or making a decision with evidence.

**Attach:** the decision, must-have constraints, location/date if relevant, and what would change your choice.

## 1. Research for a decision

```text
Help me decide [decision] for [use case].
Must-haves: [constraints]. Nice-to-haves: [preferences].
Location and time frame: [if relevant].

Search for current evidence. Prefer primary sources for product behavior,
technical claims, prices, and policies. Compare only realistic options.
Open the important sources rather than relying only on search snippets.
Record dates when freshness changes the decision.

Deliver a short recommendation, a comparison table, tradeoffs,
uncertainties, and direct links beside the claims they support.
Distinguish source claims, your inferences, and facts you could not verify.
Do not invent pricing, quotes, citations, or capabilities.
```

## 2. Extract reusable lessons from a video

```text
Analyze this video using its accessible transcript and linked materials:
[URL or provided transcript].
Find practical techniques I can reuse for [kind of work].
For each, capture: technique, when it helps, required attachments/tools,
a reusable prompt outline, the demonstrated example, and limitations.
Include timestamps only if available in the source.
Check the creator's linked repositories and distinguish demonstrations
from claims. If the video or transcript is unavailable, say so and
work only from the material you can actually access.
```

## 3. Turn findings into a useful collection

```text
Curate these sources for [audience]. Group by the job they help complete.
For each resource, give a direct source link, what it provides,
when to use it, prerequisites, limitations, and verification status.
Deduplicate mirrors and prefer original authors.
Include a short starter path instead of recommending everything at once.
Link to third-party material; do not copy full libraries without
checking the actual license and preserving required attribution.
```

**Check:** citations support the actual claims; comparisons use compatible units and plans; dates are clear; unknowns remain unknown; recommendations follow the constraints.
